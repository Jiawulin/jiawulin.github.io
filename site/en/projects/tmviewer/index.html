About the project

A media browser for local photos and videos. From an infinite canvas to a focused grid, rediscover the moments scattered across your folders.

Add local folders to browse photos and videos in one place. TianMu Viewer builds a local, read-only index without moving your originals. The web experience uses a separate demo library to showcase browsing and interactions.
